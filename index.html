Our Mission

We turn a love of books into real relief for real patients.

Story Strong is a Treasure Valley nonprofit that raises funds for patient supportive care through events built around the thing that brings our community together: a good story.

01 — Gather

Book-themed events

From literary trivia nights to full immersive mysteries, every event is designed around a story worth showing up for.

02 — Fund

Patient supportive care

Proceeds go directly toward the practical and emotional support that helps patients and families through treatment.

03 — Connect

Community, chapter by chapter

We believe fundraising should feel like a night out with neighbors, not an obligation — so people keep coming back.

Our Story

Started by someone who knows how much a good story can carry you.

Story Strong was founded by Brianna, a Treasure Valley biology graduate whose work alongside patients facing serious illness showed her how much comfort — and how much strength — people draw from story. She built Story Strong to turn that idea into something communal: events people genuinely want to attend, with proceeds that quietly go to work supporting patients through some of their hardest chapters.

What started as a single event has grown into an ongoing effort — one book-themed gathering at a time — powered almost entirely by neighbors showing up for neighbors.

— Brianna, Founder & President
